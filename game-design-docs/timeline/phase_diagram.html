Bars run Monday to Sunday. Diamonds are Sunday reviews with a tagged build. Tracks run in parallel; owners are accountable, not exclusive.

How nine weeks works

World geometry is generated from real maps, so the team spends its time on systems and story. Systems land first because the story is told through them. Karachi (half the game) is built before Delhi. Every Sunday ends with a playable build.

Cut line, in order, if a milestone slips

  1. Side content: tea stall, cricket, Kasur trip
  2. Act 3 farm and route in as cinematics
  3. Delhi hubs reduced to playable interiors
  4. Crowds limited to 12 archetypes
  5. Part 2 (Wapsi) as a later release
