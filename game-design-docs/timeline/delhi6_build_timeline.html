DHURANDHAR: THE GAME

Part 1 · Katora   /   Part 2 · Wapsi

A Delhi gangster turned beggar, recruited because he is invisible, sent into Karachi's underworld under a dead man's name. Six acts, 14 to 18 hours, built in Unreal Engine 5 from real streets.

Timeline

Six phases, one year

Systems first, because the story is told through them: money, stamina, suspicion, falls. Then Karachi, which is half the game, then Delhi, then the acts that connect them.

Tools, rules, and Karachi

Phase 01

Foundation

10 Nov – 06 Dec 2026 · 27 days

Engine, pipeline and tools

  • Unreal Engine 5.8.4, C++ project, Git LFS, CI
  • Map pipeline: real footprints to modeled streets
  • Unreal MCP for live editing
  • Terrain, weather and crowd data imported

First lit street in Unreal

Phase 02

Core systems

07 Dec – 28 Feb 2027 · 84 days

The rules the story depends on

  • Money ledger, health without regeneration
  • Stamina and the disease curve, coughing fits
  • Hidden suspicion, notebook map, daily saves
  • Falls, rail heights, ballistics, katta misfire
  • Calendar, weather, crowds, traffic

One in-game day playable end to end

Phase 03

Karachi · Act 4

01 Mar – 30 May 2027 · 91 days

Half the game: Gulbahar Lines

  • District built from the Lyari slice
  • Shrine, tankers, hawala shops, gambling den
  • Sattar, Shahzaib, Qureshi; the branch choice
  • The Rangers raid escape without a weapon

Scope and team

The whole story, with a cut list ready

The plan builds all six acts. If a phase slips, cut from the right-hand list first, in that order, so the story itself never loses a beat.

Ships

Both parts, six acts

  • Ghaziabad, Nizamuddin, Karachi, the creek
  • Money, stamina, disease, suspicion systems
  • The Sattar or Shahzaib branch
  • The balcony, the rewind, the coda
  • 60 fps on an RTX 3070, 18+ rating
Cut first if late

Cut list

  • 1 · Side content (tea stall, cricket, Kasur trip)
  • 2 · Act 3 farm as a short cutscene
  • 3 · Smaller Delhi hubs, Karachi stays full
  • 4 · Fewer crowd character types
  • 5 · Ship Part 2 (Wapsi) as a second release
Team

Who does what

  • Tarun · story, script, direction, playtests
  • Rayan · engine, physics, crowds, performance, builds
  • Claude · generators, C++ and Python, imports, research, docs
  • Hire · voice, performance capture, legal review
