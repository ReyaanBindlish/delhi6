Timeline

Five phases to launch

Each phase ends with something playable. After a short first week, phases run Monday to Sunday, two weeks each, so every phase closes with a Sunday review and a build.

Scope and team

What "complete" means on 10 January

Two months is enough for one polished, playable district. The rest of Old Delhi and South Delhi come after launch as updates, using the same pipeline.

In version 1.0

Chandni Chowk slice

  • 500 m around Chandni Chowk, Dariba and Kinari
  • Four weathers and a full day–night cycle
  • Crowds, rickshaws, birds, street sound
  • A short Diwali-week story, 3 to 5 missions
  • 60 fps on an RTX 3070
After launch

Updates

  • The full walled city (2.9 × 2.2 km)
  • All 52 character types
  • Hero landmarks from photogrammetry
  • South Delhi: Lajpat, Sarojini, GK, Hauz Khas
  • More stories and festivals
Team

Who does what

  • Rayan · art direction, story, reference photos and sound from Delhi
  • Co-developer · engine, physics, crowds, performance, builds
  • Claude · generators, C++ and Python, imports, research, docs
  • Together · weekly review, playtests, launch
